## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary (enterprise B2B):** operations managers, factory/plant owners, IT admins, and procurement at mid-size enterprises — factories, warehouses, offices, educational institutions, hospitals, retail chains — in Anand, Nadiad, and across Gujarat, who need professional surveillance designed, installed, and maintained.
- **Secondary (small B2B / B2C):** shop owners, clinics, builders, and homeowners in Anand–Nadiad who want reliable CCTV without enterprise complexity.

## Product Purpose

Shravonix is a security-systems integration business: it designs, installs, and maintains CCTV/surveillance systems and offers annual maintenance contracts (AMC) with monitoring support. The website's job is to win enterprise inquiries (site survey → quote) and establish Shravonix as an engineering-led alternative to typical local CCTV dealers.

## Positioning

Engineering-led surveillance: "Signal over noise." The founder is a practicing DevOps/infrastructure engineer — camera placement, network design, storage math, and remote monitoring are engineered, not guesswork. A local Gujarat team with enterprise-grade discipline is a pairing a typical CCTV shop cannot truthfully copy.

## Operating Context

- Based in Anand and Nadiad, Gujarat, India; serves those cities plus surrounding Gujarat.
- Sales flow for B2B: inquiry → free site survey → system design & quote → installation → AMC.
- B2C flow is simpler: inquiry → direct quote → install.
- The business is new as a brand; project photos and client names exist but have not been supplied yet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed services: CCTV/surveillance systems (design + installation) and AMC & monitoring.
- Not confirmed (do not ship until stated): access control, fire alarms, intrusion alarms, intercoms.
